How it works

Live weather (Open-Meteo) → sunlight spectrum (SPECTRL2, scaled to measured direct and diffuse light) → cell temperature → physics simulation of all 231 mixes in 5% steps.

Accuracy = share of 500 simulations, each with the light, temperature and pigment settings nudged within their uncertainty, in which that mix reaches at least 85% of the best mix. Same location and time always give the same result.
